    What these records mean

    Wikidata’s filming location property describes where a production was shot. It is different from where its story takes place. Coverage is uneven: a missing film is not evidence it was never filmed here, and many records identify only a municipality. Follow the linked statement to inspect its references.

    Release years use the earliest release date listed in Wikidata and may be missing. Film/television labels use available TMDb identifier properties. These fields are discovery aids, not a complete filmography. Recorded campuses, parks and studios may cover substantial areas or be closed to the public.

    Privacy: filtering the downloaded catalogue stays in your browser. Searching another place sends that text to Wikidata; choosing it sends a Wikidata ID to its query service. Film-detail requests send only a Wikidata production ID through CoolCode to TMDb. Map tiles contact OpenStreetMap; posters contact TMDb. Saved outings remain on this device.
